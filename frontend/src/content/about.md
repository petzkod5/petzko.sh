# Welcome and Hello, I'm Dylan

I build, run, and manage infrastructure for fun and for a living. You're currently connected to my homelab, Rivendell!
Rivendell is a hybrid edge + cloud homelab built on Talos, GitOps and an unreasonable amount of observability for such
a small setup.

By day I work on backend, frontend, platform, and all around every type of system imaginable. If someone has a problem - I usually can create a solution for it. Outside of work and tech you can find me getting beat up on the regular via jui-jitsu, brewing my own beer and mead, or going on long walks on the beach with my dog (I don't own a dog). In my career I've worked often in confidential and classified environments doing work for healthcare systems and defense systems. I've luckily have had the opportunity to travel the world for my work and I love every minute of what I do.

Also - My biggest strength is that I know how to exit vim.
